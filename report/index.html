What to fix first

The site has no indexing blocker: no article is noindexed and every canonical is self-referencing. The biggest gains come from a handful of template fixes, then refreshing the high-demand older articles.

  1. Put every article in the sitemap and make blog pagination crawlable (S1, S2). 140 articles are missing from sitemap.xml and the /blog page numbers are buttons without links. 60 articles have no links from any other article, and 40 of those are not in the sitemap either, so Google has almost no path to them.
  2. Add BlogPosting schema, og:type=article and a visible "Updated" date (S4, S8, S9). One template change covers all 240 articles and makes every refresh you do visible to Google.
  3. Stop lazy-loading the hero image (S5). It is the largest element on every article, so this is the quickest page-speed win.
  4. Fix the 104 articles linking to old /service/* URLs and the 41 broken links (S6, Broken links sheet).
  5. Refresh the top of the ranking below. Articles such as Ember.js, Framer website builder, Backbone.js, Gatsby and Svelte rank on page 1-2 with 150k-830k impressions but are 20-25 months old, out of the sitemap and get very few clicks.

Removed articles also need attention: /blog/ruby-on-rails is live (358 clicks) but its uppercase twin returns a soft 404, and /blog/styling-strategies-in-next-js (135 clicks) now 307-redirects to the blog index. See "Removed and redirected URLs".

Checks that passed

  • Indexability: no live article is noindexed; there is no X-Robots-Tag; every article returns 200 at its own URL.
  • Canonicals: present, absolute and self-referencing on every live article.
  • Meta descriptions: present on every article (45 are over 165 characters, 12 under 100; see per-article issues).
  • H1: exactly one on 238 of 239 checked articles (the Tailwind CSS 4 responsive design article has two).
  • Social: og:title, og:description, og:image (1200×630) and twitter:card=summary_large_image on every article.
  • Mobile: viewport meta and lang="en" on every article.
  • Server: median time to first byte 0.45 s, gzip, CDN caching (s-maxage=3600), HSTS, nosniff and X-Frame-Options set. www and trailing-slash URLs redirect correctly (301 and 308).
  • Images: every content image has an alt attribute, and Prismic serves WebP/AVIF automatically. The issues are empty alts (33 images), file-name-style alts (66) and missing dimensions.

Method and limits

  • Article list: Search Console pages, sitemap.xml and the /blog listing combined. 244 article URLs were found: 240 live and 4 removed or redirected.
  • Links: 1,722 unique link targets and 963 images were checked. Facebook, X and LinkedIn share links block automated checks and were skipped. 95 outbound links returned 403, 429 or similar (bot protection, e.g. ThemeForest and Adobe) and 8 timed out or failed a certificate check, so they were not counted as broken.
  • Page speed: Core Web Vitals were not measured because the PageSpeed Insights API was over its daily quota. Speed findings come from the HTML: lazy hero image, image weight and dimensions, payload size and server timing. Check the Core Web Vitals report in Search Console to confirm field data.
  • /blog/ruby-on-rails: its saved HTML was overwritten by the uppercase /ruby-on-Rails page (the Mac's disk treats both names as the same file). Its title, meta, canonical and schema need a manual check.
  • Word count covers the article body only, from the H1 to the share block, including the FAQ.
